About StudySource

Research is difficult enough. Organizing it should feel simple.

StudySource is a student research and reading organizer designed to keep sources, personal notes, handwritten work, and deadlines together.

WHY

The problem we are solving

Students often collect journal links in browser tabs, book details in documents, handwritten ideas in notebooks, and deadlines in separate calendars. Important material becomes difficult to find precisely when it is needed.

StudySource gives each research project a clear folder where these materials can be collected without automatically writing or interpreting the student's work.

01

Student controlled

Students choose what to save and write every research note themselves.

02

Source transparent

Every saved reference keeps its original link and clearly states what content is available.

03

Privacy conscious

Each student's folders, notes, images, and deadlines belong only to their account.

Built with purpose

Five services, each with one clear job.
